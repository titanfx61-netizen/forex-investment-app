Privacy Policy

At TITAN FX TRADING, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal and financial information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your data when you use our platform, automated trading bots, and investment services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our trading services. This includes:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, and country of residence provided during registration and KYC verification.
  • Financial Information: Cryptocurrency wallet addresses, bank account details (for wire transfers), and transaction history.
  • Trading Credentials: MetaTrader 5 (MT5) login credentials, account numbers, and server details provided to connect our automated bots to your trading account. These are encrypted and stored securely.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and usage logs to ensure platform security and prevent fraud.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • To create, manage, and verify your trading account.
  • To execute automated trading strategies via connected MT5 accounts.
  • To process deposits, withdrawals, and calculate investment returns.
  • To communicate important updates, security alerts, and platform notifications.
  • To comply with legal obligations and prevent fraudulent activities.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. All sensitive information, including MT5 credentials and financial details, is encrypted in transit and at rest. We utilize secure database infrastructure (Supabase) and regularly audit our systems for vulnerabilities. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

We may share minimal necessary data with trusted third-party providers to operate our platform, such as:

  • Database & Authentication: Supabase for secure data storage and user management.
  • Market Data: TradingView for live charting and market analysis.
  • Payment Processors: Blockchain networks and banking partners for processing deposits and withdrawals.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to external marketers or unauthorized third parties.

5. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements for financial records).
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing at any time.

To exercise these rights, please contact our support team via the platform.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us through the Support Center within your dashboard.

By using the TITAN FX TRADING platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
